About

I spent 7+ years helping brands grow through content, strategy, and execution. Somewhere along the way I stopped doing the repetitive parts by hand and started building the systems that do them automatically.

Today I design and ship AI-powered automation: conversational agents with real tool access, CRM data pipelines, scheduled monitoring and escalation bots, and LLM workflows for scoring and reporting. The goal is always the same — cut execution time without cutting quality.

I'm also a qualified lawyer, which is less of a detour than it sounds: it taught me to read systems carefully, reason through edge cases, and care about getting the details exactly right.

I like messy problems, clear outcomes, and a few agents doing the heavy lifting.

AI Operations Assistant

A chat-native AI assistant for a sales org drowning in "what's the status of X?" lookups across CRM, call data, and the web.

Integration · CRM

CRM Tag → Field Enrichment

The CRM couldn't natively extract structured data (partner name, deal value) buried inside free-text contact tags.

Built: a secured webhook microservice that parses tags with regex, normalizes names (title-case, entity suffixes), and writes clean values back to CRM custom fields via its API.

↳ Automated data entry that was previously manual — or impossible natively.

Node.jsExpressREST APIWebhooks
Automation · SLA

Pipeline SLA Escalation Bot

Deals were stalling in pipeline stages with no accountability and no one flagged in time.

Built: a cron-driven monitor tracking stage age against an SLA ladder (1h/2h notify → 3h/6h escalate), posting alerts to team chat, with atomic day-locks so nothing double-fires. Timezone-aware.

↳ Reliable, de-duplicated escalations and clear ownership.

Node.jsnode-cronChat webhooksAtomic locking
LLM · Analytics

Call Scoring & Analytics Dashboard

No objective, consistent way to score sales calls at scale — quality was a matter of opinion.

Built: an LLM grader that scores call transcripts against a defined rubric, persists the results, and surfaces them in a token-gated web dashboard with per-rep rollups.

↳ Consistent, queryable call-quality metrics for every rep.

Node.jsLLM gradingSupabaseHTML · JS

Built: a pipeline that ingests call transcripts, chunks and embeds them into a vector store, and serves relevant context back to the assistant at query time.

↳ Answers grounded in real institutional history, not guesses.
